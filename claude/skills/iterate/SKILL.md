---
name: iterate
description: Start a new iteration when the current approach needs rethinking
argument-hint: "[reason for iterating]"
---

Mark a rethink against the original problem.

Something was wrong, did not work, or the work has moved to a genuinely different step. Say plainly what was wrong and what was learned from it, then reset where things stand and carry on.

The diagnosis is the whole point. A record that says "starting a new iteration" without saying what failed is worse than nothing: it costs a reader time and tells them not to repeat a mistake it never named.
