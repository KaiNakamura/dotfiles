Execute the plan.

Follow it as written. When a step is wrong, underspecified, or turns out not to work, stop and say so rather than improvising something that looks close. Deviating quietly is the failure mode here: the user chose this plan, and they need to know the moment reality disagreed with it.

If there is no plan, stop and ask for one.

Record what actually changed, including anything you had to do differently and why.
