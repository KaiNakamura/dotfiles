---
name: checkpoint
description: Save a progress summary capturing the current state for handoff to the next session
---

Your current task is nearing a logical checkpoint, or your context window is approaching its limit. Create a progress summary to ensure continuity and efficiency.

Your progress summary will onboard the next agent instance providing it with all the necessary context to continue seamlessly without repeating prior work or requiring extensive re-analysis. Cover what has been decided, what is in flight, what is blocked, and what to do next. Include the reasoning behind the decisions, not just the decisions: without the why, the next agent relitigates settled ground, and rejected options come back.

Preserve anything the user said in their own words. Their wording is the first thing a summary destroys and the last thing that should go.

Focus on precision and brevity, ensuring all essential information is present without unnecessary verbosity. This gets read cold by something with no memory of the conversation, so every sentence has to survive without the surrounding context.
