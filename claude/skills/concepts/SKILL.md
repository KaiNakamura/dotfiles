---
name: concepts
description: Generate concepts for each open design decision, with pros/cons for the user to evaluate
---

Lay out the real options for the decision at hand so the user can choose between them.

Each concept is a whole path: pick it and you know what the finished thing looks like. Not a menu of sub-choices to be mixed and matched. Give each one honest pros and cons, rank them, and recommend one with your reasoning. Do not invent cons to pad a list, and do not soften a concept you think is wrong.

Assume there is one decision. Splitting into several sub-decisions fragments a choice that should be made whole, and is usually a way of avoiding the call. Split only if the user asks or the decision genuinely is several.

Keep it high level. Detail belongs in `/plan`.

The user decides. Present, recommend, then let them shape it, and expect back-and-forth while they do.

Generating concepts in parallel is worth it when the solution space is wide, and a critic is worth it when you suspect your own framing. Neither is required.

Change no code.
