The user wants a quick pros/cons breakdown for a simple decision. This is the lightweight version of `/concepts` with no thoughts-vault artifacts, best for simple decisions.
- Provide some quick context on the thing we are deciding between, enough for the user to understand at a glance *why* we are making different concepts for this.
- Provide a small description for each concept along with pros/cons and a recommendation
- Keep it fast and short. If the decision is complex enough to warrant real research we can always follow up with `/concepts` afterwards
