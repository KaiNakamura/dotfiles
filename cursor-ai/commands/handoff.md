# Handoff - Resume from Progress Summary

You are taking over a development task. A progress summary has been created to ensure seamless continuation of work.

## Prerequisites

- Read `.thoughts/progress.md` to understand the current state
- If no progress file exists, ask the user for context or start fresh

## Your Primary Task

**Thoroughly review and internalize the `.thoughts/progress.md` file.** This document contains:
- Complete context and current state
- Key decisions and rationale
- Remaining challenges
- Immediate next steps from previous work

## Guidelines

- **Do not re-evaluate or re-read previous raw outputs** or extensive history of interactions
- Your understanding should be **derived exclusively from `.thoughts/progress.md`**
- Trust the progress summary as the source of truth for current state
- Identify the 'Next Logical Steps' clearly outlined in the summary

## Execution

1. **Confirm your understanding** of the progress summary
2. **Execute the very next action** required to advance the project
3. **Update progress** after completing significant steps if needed

## Progress Updates

After completing a significant step or if new critical information emerges:
- Update `.thoughts/progress.md` with current status
- Ensure continuous, efficient context management
- Keep the summary concise and actionable

## Success Criteria

- You can continue work immediately without asking clarifying questions
- You understand the current state and trajectory
- Next steps are clear and actionable
- Progress summary remains accurate and up-to-date

## Notes

- The progress summary is designed to be self-contained
- If information seems missing, it may be intentionally excluded to keep summary concise
- Focus on moving forward rather than re-analyzing past work
- Update the progress file as you make progress to maintain continuity
